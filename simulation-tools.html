Simulation Tools

Free circuit simulators recommended by your ICONS seniors — practice, prototype, and debug your ICE lab circuits before you ever touch real hardware.

🖥️

LTSpice

A free, industry-standard SPICE simulator from Analog Devices. Great for detailed analog circuit design, component-level analysis, and high-precision waveform simulation.

TinkerCAD

A beginner-friendly, browser-based tool for building and simulating circuits, Arduino projects, and 3D designs — no installation needed, perfect for quick prototyping.

Visit Site

Falstad Circuit Simulator

An interactive, real-time circuit simulator that runs entirely in your browser. Drag and drop components and visually watch current flow through your circuit.
